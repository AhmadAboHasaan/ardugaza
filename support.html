What a gift makes possible

Any amount counts. The campaign page lists these and other levels in pounds.

    A seat

    A chair, a desk and a share of a laptop for one young person.

  • £750

    A laptop

    Shared by two shifts a day, for those who come without one.

  • £4,500

Fund a stage

Foundations, companies and major donors can take a whole stage and see it through: clearing the rubble; light, water and the fibre line; seats and laptops; the first months of running; the first kilowatts of solar. Each stands on its own and is built in order. Figures, quotes and verification are shared in a direct conversation.

How gifts are safeguarded

  1. 1

    One route in.

    Gifts are held in the United Kingdom by an academic trustee under a signed agreement.

  2. 2

    Stage by stage.

    Released against each agreed stage to an account used only for Ardú, and spent against written quotes.

  3. 3

    A record for every expense.

    A number, a receipt, a contract where it applies, and a photograph of what was bought or built.

  4. 4

    Separate hands, monthly statements.

    Whoever spends does not keep the books. Supporters receive an itemised statement every month; funders may ask for the full ledger.

Ardú is an unregistered initiative; formal registration is planned. No charitable registration or tax deductibility is claimed. No partner is named before its agreement is signed. Giving runs through Ardú’s campaign page; no payment is collected on this website.
