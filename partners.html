Bring what money cannot buy.

A hall in Gaza City with two hundred young people a day, power that stays on and internet that holds, ready for the programmes you already run and the opportunities you can open.

Why partner

  • A ready audience

    Two hundred motivated young adults a day, selected on published criteria, in one place, with the connectivity to join your programme live.

  • A place that works

    Deliver in person or remotely into a room with power, internet, screens and a team on the ground to run the floor.

  • Reporting you can use

    Attendance, completion and outcomes reported each cycle, with the data your own funders ask for.

What a partner can bring

  • A course or curriculum, delivered in cycles
  • Trainers and mentors, in person or online
  • Laptops and equipment
  • Jobs, internships and freelance clients for the people who come out of Ardú
  • Certificates and pathways into further study
  • Your voice: tell your network what is rising in Gaza City
Partner with us

How it works

No partner is named publicly before its agreement is signed.

  1. 1

    A conversation

    Write to us with what you can bring. We reply within a week.

  2. 2

    A short written agreement

    What each side provides, when, and how it is reported.

  3. 3

    The first cycle

    Scheduled into Ardú’s calendar, announced to participants, run with our team on the floor.

  4. 4

    Reporting

    Each cycle closes with attendance, completion and outcomes.

For funders and institutions

Foundations, companies and institutional funders can take a whole stage, from clearing the rubble to the first kilowatts of solar, and follow it through with full figures and reporting. The complete plan, with stages, costs, verification, the energy design and the earned-income model, is shared on request, together with the location of the hall.
