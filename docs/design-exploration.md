Public journal uses Bodoni Moda, intimate portrait slots, rose and paper, asymmetrical articles and quiet image reveals. Its dependence on real photography is an immediate weakness.

Power to work uses Barlow Condensed, blue-black with icy blue, routed diagrams, large uppercase statements and linear process transitions. It explains infrastructure well but underrepresents the people.

Open frame uses Manrope, off-white, charcoal and citron, broad editorial columns, real planned-seat counts, open document-like funding rows and a finite energy-path reveal. Selected for its balance of specificity, warmth and credibility. Photography slots can later replace diagrams without changing the content structure. A grid diagram is explicitly schematic, never a hall floor plan.

## Impeccable challengers
Accretion disk, curved paper shell, algorave, particle detector, earthy coffeehouse and pastel character sheet were assessed against audience identification and product clarity. All lose both axes when applied to the institutional funding task; none supplies a more truthful or legible carrier. Their useful disciplines raise the selected direction: coherent focus (accretion), structural honesty (paper), synchronized finite motion (algorave), selectable textual explanations (detector), warmth in body copy (coffeehouse), and a consistent graphic vocabulary (character sheet). Their decorative motifs are not adopted.
